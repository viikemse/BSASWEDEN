BSA Sweden

Three Studies Hall — A platform for Chinese Buddhists and traditional culture in Sweden

About Us

A self-organized and self-managed platform for life and Buddhist study, mainly for Chinese people in Sweden.

We study Buddhist sutras, Chinese traditional culture, share practical knowledge about life and law in Sweden, and organize meditation activities.

Officially registered as a non-profit organization in February 2018 (BSA SWEDEN), with nearly a hundred members so far.
