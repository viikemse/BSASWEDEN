Meditation Center

Daily Life Practice and Advanced Buddhist Meditation

Three Studies Meditation Center

Planned programs include:

  • Introductory Meditation & Study Class
  • Advanced Meditation Class (requires prior foundation in Buddhist study and practice)

Membership-based. Primarily in-person retreats, with online options when necessary.

Members are encouraged to do at least 7 days of retreat per year.

We mainly follow the Buddhist teachings transmitted by Khenpo Sodargye Rinpoche.
