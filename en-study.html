Systematic Learning of Buddhist Teachings

Three Studies Society

We are committed to the systematic study and practice of Buddhism using a formal online university-style teaching model.

Main Courses:

  • Foundation Class (6 months): Buddhist perspectives on daily life issues, “The Teaching on the Two Entrances and Four Practices”, “The Thirty-Seven Practices of a Bodhisattva”, etc.
  • Advanced Class (approx. 3 years): “The Way of the Bodhisattva”, “The Words of My Perfect Teacher”, etc.

Voluntary registration with freedom to join or leave, but with certain learning requirements and assessments. Classes are usually held once a week (2-3 hours). Participants are required to preview videos, audios and texts before class.

Free to join. Managed by class groups.
